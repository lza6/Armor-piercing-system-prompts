# Skill 手绘动效完整版

## Style Prompt
9:16 中文知识讲解。暖白纸面、粗而略不规则的深色线条、蓝衣办公人物和黄色小机器人。通过独立元素的移动、铰链式手势、书页与步骤组合表达含义。保持轻松、清晰，不模仿博主个人 IP。

## Colors
- Paper #F8F6EF
- Ink #24251F
- Cobalt #2855C7
- Sunflower #F0C541
- Tomato #D64B36

## Typography
Microsoft YaHei for Chinese. Georgia for large Skill wordmark. Chinese text remains deterministic and separate from illustration.

## Motion
36-second complete narration. Eight scenes: repeated requests, organizing instructions, definition, weekly report procedure, supporting materials, reuse, permissions and checking, final definition. Paper crossfades between scenes. Character forearm rotates independently; document components move independently. Hold the final definition after the narration. Do not imply precise lip sync.

## What NOT to Do
- No full-image pan/zoom as the principal animation.
- No AI-generated text or new voice.
- No dark background, gradients, or drifting paper color.
- No decorative motion that obscures the explanation.
- No promise of photorealistic or fully articulated character animation.
