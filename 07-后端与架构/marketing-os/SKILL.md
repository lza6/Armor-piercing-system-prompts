# Marketing Skills OS

## Mandatory Content Standards

- Match output length to the request. Keep routing concise and expand complete plans.
- Use active voice, short paragraphs, direct language, and clear tables.
- Address the reader as you and your.
- Separate evidence, assumptions, decisions, and recommendations.
- Do not invent metrics, customer facts, or platform capabilities.
- Use no em dashes, hashtags, emojis, or filler closings.
- End each major section with a specific next step.

## Purpose

Use this skill as the routing layer for broad marketing requests. It connects context, strategy, demand, conversion, pipeline, retention, measurement, and asset production. It does not replace specialist skills.

Before routing, read the available product context. Accept `.marketing-os/product-context.md`, `.agents/product-marketing.md`, or a user-provided context file. Treat blank templates as missing context.

## Context Readiness Gate

Require these minimum fields before producing a full plan or campaign.

| Field | Required Information |
|---|---|
| Product | Name, category, offer, and current stage. |
| Audience | ICP, buyer role, geography, and knowledge level. |
| Goal | One measurable business outcome. |
| Proof | Customer evidence, usage data, testimonials, or stated assumptions. |
| Constraints | Budget, timeline, team, channels, and compliance limits. |

If context is incomplete, route to `product-marketing` and ask one focused question at a time.

## Operating Sequence

1. Confirm context and the decision the work must support.
2. Select one primary outcome.
3. Choose one primary skill and no more than three supporting skills.
4. Read the relevant tool guides before recommending platform actions.
5. Define the asset, owner, deadline, metric, and review checkpoint.
6. Produce the work and identify assumptions.
7. Schedule a review based on the selected metric.

- Do not send outreach, publish content, change ad budgets, or alter customer data without explicit approval.
- Require consent and suppression checks for prospecting and SMS.
- Keep secrets out of output and run records.
- Mark estimates and unverified claims clearly.
