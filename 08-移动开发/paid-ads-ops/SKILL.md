# Paid Ads Operations

## Mandatory Content Standards

- Use account data and unit economics before recommending spend changes.
- Separate platform facts, estimates, assumptions, and decisions.
- Never recommend publishing or changing budgets without approval.
- Use active voice, short paragraphs, direct language, and clear tables.
- Use no em dashes, hashtags, emojis, or filler closings.
- End every section with a measurable next action.

## Required Inputs

Collect the offer, audience, funnel event, platform, account export, date range, spend, revenue or lead value, conversion definition, landing page, creative set, target CPA or ROAS, budget limits, and tracking status.

## Operating Modes

| Mode | Purpose | Core Output |
|---|---|---|
| Audit | Find waste, tracking gaps, structural issues, and scaling opportunities. | Prioritized account audit. |
| Plan | Define platform, structure, budget, audience, creative, and timeline. | Campaign plan. |
| Budget | Evaluate allocation, bid strategy, CPA, ROAS, and break-even point. | Budget decision table. |
| Competitor | Study public ads, claims, offers, and creative patterns. | Intelligence brief. |
| Landing | Check message match, speed, trust, form, and mobile flow. | Post-click action list. |
| Creative | Create angles, hooks, variants, and test cells. | Creative brief. |

## Core Calculations

Use CPA as spend divided by conversions. Use ROAS as attributed revenue divided by spend. Use break-even CPA as gross profit per conversion after variable costs. State the attribution window and whether revenue is gross or net.

## Review Framework

Check tracking first. Then check economics, account structure, audience, search terms or placements, creative fatigue, frequency, landing page message match, and follow-up quality. Rank actions by expected impact and confidence.

## Output Format

| Finding | Evidence | Risk or Opportunity | Recommendation | Expected Metric | Owner | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Follow with platform notes, budget model, campaign structure, creative matrix, landing page fixes, experiment plan, and approval gates.
